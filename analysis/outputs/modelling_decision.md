# Modelling decision

The annual trend regressions in stage 04 are descriptive statistical models.
No additional predictive ML model is fitted. The file has no independent
innovation/impact target, no text or citation outcomes, and only 14 comparable
annual points per company. Predicting an AI flag from its component scores or
other AI flags would reproduce its deterministic construction and leak the
target. A random patent split would also ignore years and possible patent
families. Forecasts beyond 2023 would require newer data, stable entity coverage
and a temporal evaluation design. Clustering the supplied classifier scores
would mostly repackage known technology labels without validating invention.

Reasonable alternatives: a time-series forecasting study with longer
comparable histories, manual-label validation with document/family-separated
splits, or impact modelling with forward citations and a fixed citation window.
None can be validly evaluated from the present extract alone.
